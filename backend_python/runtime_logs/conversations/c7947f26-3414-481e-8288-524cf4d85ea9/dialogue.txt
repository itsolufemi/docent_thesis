This is a fascinating piece by Edwin Landseer, who was incredibly successful and a favorite of Queen Victoria. It's a rare example of him tackling "Orientalist" themes, which were very fashionable at the time.

If you look closely, there's an interesting detail in the composition: the animals are arranged by intelligence. At the bottom, you have the Arab mare and her foal, and as your eye moves up the painting, you'll see two Persian greyhounds and then two monkeys—one of whom is even wearing an earring.

It's also one of the most expensive paintings the founder of the collection, Sir Richard Wallace, ever bought. He purchased it from the Prince of Wales for £6,300, which was a huge sum.
