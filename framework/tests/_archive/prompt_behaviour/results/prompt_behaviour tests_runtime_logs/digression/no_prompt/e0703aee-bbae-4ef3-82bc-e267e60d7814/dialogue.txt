Well, Landseer was actually the most fashionable painter of the early Victorian era, and he was a huge favourite of Queen Victoria herself. He's mostly famous for his animal paintings, but this one is quite unusual because it's a rare example of him tackling those "Orientalist" themes that were so trendy at the time.
