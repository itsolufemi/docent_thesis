Text:
So, this one is actually a bit of a rarity for Landseer, who mostly did animals in more traditional settings. The point of the painting is really to lean into that "Orientalist" fashion of the time—basically a Western fantasy of the Middle East. He tells the story through a hierarchy of animals; if you look from the bottom up, they get more "intelligent," starting with the mare and foal, then the greyhounds, and finally the monkeys at the top. Oh, and if you look over on the right, you'll see two pipes in a jar, which is Landseer's way of hinting that the owner is nearby without actually painting them.
