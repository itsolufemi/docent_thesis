Well, this one's actually quite a rarity for Landseer. He was the absolute toast of the Victorian era, but he didn't usually dive into 'Orientalist' themes like this—which was the big fashion at the time. 

The painting is really a study in intelligence and status. If you look at the animals, there's a bit of a hierarchy going on; the further up the canvas you look, from the mare and foal at the bottom to the monkeys at the top, the more intelligent the animals get. It's a clever way of structuring the scene, right?
