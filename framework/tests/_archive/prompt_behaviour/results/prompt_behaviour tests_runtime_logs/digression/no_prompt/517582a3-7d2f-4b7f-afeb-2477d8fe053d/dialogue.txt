Well, the most unusual thing here is actually the way Landseer has arranged the animals. If you look at them, the "intelligence" of the animals increases as you move up the painting—starting with the mare and foal at the bottom, then the greyhounds, and finally the monkeys at the top. Text:
Well, they're actually worlds apart in terms of what they're trying to do. While the tent scene is a bit of a playful study of animals, *The Rising of the Sun* is a grand, theatrical allegory. It tells the story of the sun god, Apollo, setting out in his chariot to bring light to the world, with the nymph Tethys helping him along. It's much more about power and prestige—it was actually designed as a model for a tapestry to decorate King Louis XV's bedroom.
