Well, the way Landseer has placed the animals is actually a bit of a clever play on intelligence. If you look at the painting, you'll notice that the higher up the canvas an animal is positioned, the more intelligent it's meant to be. So, you've got the mare and foal at the bottom, then the greyhounds, and finally the monkeys at the top—one of whom is even wearing an earring.
