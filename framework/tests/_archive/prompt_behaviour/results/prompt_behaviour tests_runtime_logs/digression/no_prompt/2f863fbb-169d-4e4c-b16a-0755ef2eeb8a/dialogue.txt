Text:
Well, this one is actually a bit of a rarity for Landseer, who usually stuck to more traditional English scenes. It's basically a study in "Orientalism," which was really fashionable back then. He's telling a story of luxury and hierarchy here; if you look at the animals, they actually get "more intelligent" the higher up the canvas they are—starting with the mare and foal at the bottom, then the greyhounds, and finally the monkeys at the top.
