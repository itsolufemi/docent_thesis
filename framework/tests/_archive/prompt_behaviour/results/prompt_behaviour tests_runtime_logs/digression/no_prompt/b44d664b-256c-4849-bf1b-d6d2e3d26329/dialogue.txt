Well, this is a really interesting piece by Landseer. It's basically a study in "Orientalism," which was very trendy back then, showing a scene with an Arab mare and her foal under a tent. He’s actually telling a bit of a story through the animals—if you look, they're arranged by intelligence, from the horses at the bottom up to the monkeys at the top.
