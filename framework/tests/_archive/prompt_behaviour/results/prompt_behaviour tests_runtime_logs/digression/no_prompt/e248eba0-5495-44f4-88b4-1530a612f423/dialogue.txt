Text:
Well, this is actually a rare example of Landseer leaning into 'Orientalism', which was a huge trend in Victorian England. He's basically playing with the era's fascination with the 'exotic' East, using the Arab tent and animals as a way to evoke a sense of luxury and distant adventure. I think he's focusing more on the fashion and glamour of the East rather than any real cultural depth.
