Well, the real point here is that this isn't just a pretty scene of mythology; it's actually a bit of a political power play. Madame de Pompadour commissioned this to link Louis XV to the glory of the previous king, while subtly positioning herself as a key political advisor. By using Apollo, the sun god, Boucher is basically saying the King is the light of the world, and Pompadour is the one helping him get there.
