Well, I get that. On the surface, it just looks like a fancy mythological scene, right? But actually, it's more of a political statement. It was commissioned by Madame de Pompadour to subtly link King Louis XV—and her own influence over him—to the divine power and stability of the sun god, Apollo. It's basically about power and status, just dressed up in a really beautiful, airy way.
