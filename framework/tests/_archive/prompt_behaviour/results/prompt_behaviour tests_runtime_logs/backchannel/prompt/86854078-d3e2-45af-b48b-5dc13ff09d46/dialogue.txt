Well, since you're asking about status, we should look at the portrait of Margaret, Countess of Blessington. The interesting thing here is that she's actually dressed very simply with almost no jewellery, but the artist, Thomas Lawrence, tucked a huge clue in there. If you look at the chair, there's an ermine-trimmed robe draped over it, which is really the only signal of her high aristocratic rank. It's a bit of a contrast, right? I think it creates a tension between her personal simplicity and her official social standing.
