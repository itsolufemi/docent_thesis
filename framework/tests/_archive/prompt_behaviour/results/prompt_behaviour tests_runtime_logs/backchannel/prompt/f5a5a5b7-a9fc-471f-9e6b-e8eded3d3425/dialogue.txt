Well, this painting is really all about the bond between animals and their owner, though we don't actually see the owner here. Landseer uses the animals to create a kind of hierarchy of intelligence; if you look at the painting, the animals get "smarter" the higher up they are placed. So you've got the mare and foal at the bottom, then the Persian greyhounds, and finally the monkeys right at the top.
