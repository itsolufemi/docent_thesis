Well, this is actually a bit of a rarity for Landseer, as he's playing with "Orientalist" themes here. The painting is really about a hierarchy of intelligence; if you look from the bottom up, the animals get smarter, starting with the mare and foal and ending with the monkeys at the top. Oh, and if you glance over to the right, you'll see two pipes in a jar—that's how the artist lets us know the owner is nearby without actually showing him.
