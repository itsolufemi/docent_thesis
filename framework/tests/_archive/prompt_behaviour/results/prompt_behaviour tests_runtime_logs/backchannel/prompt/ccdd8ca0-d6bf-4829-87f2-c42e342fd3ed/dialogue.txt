Well, if you look at the way Landseer has arranged the animals, there's actually a bit of a clever hierarchy going on. Basically, the higher up the painting an animal is placed, the more intelligent it's meant to be. You've got the mare and foal at the bottom, then the greyhounds, and finally those monkeys right at the top—one of them is even wearing an earring, which adds to that sense of personality.
