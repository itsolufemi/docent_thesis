So, the main thing to know about this piece is that it's a bit of a rarity for Landseer—he's mostly known for his animals, but here he's leaning into "Orientalism," which was a huge fashion at the time. It's essentially a study in hierarchy; if you look at the animals, they actually get more "intelligent" the higher up the canvas they are, starting with the mare and foal and moving up to the monkeys.

As for why it matters to the collection, well, it was incredibly expensive. Sir Richard Wallace bought it from the Prince of Wales for over 6,000 pounds, which is one of the highest prices he ever paid for a single work.
