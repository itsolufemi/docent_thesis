Well, this one's actually quite a rarity for Landseer. He was the most successful painter of the early Victorian era, but he didn't usually dive into "Orientalist" themes like this. 

The real point of the piece is this sort of hierarchy of intelligence; if you look from the bottom up, the animals get smarter, starting with the mare and foal, then the greyhounds, and finally the monkeys at the top. Oh, and if you look at the jar on the right, you'll see two pipes—to me, that's a clever little nod to the owner who isn't actually in the frame.
