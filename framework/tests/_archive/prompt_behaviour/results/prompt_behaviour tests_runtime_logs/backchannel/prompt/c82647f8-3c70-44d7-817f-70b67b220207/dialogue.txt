Well, the earring is actually a little clue about the hierarchy in the painting. If you look at the animals, Landseer arranged them so that the higher up they are in the composition, the more "intelligent" they are. The monkey with the earring is right up there, showing a level of sophistication or human-like status compared to the animals below.
