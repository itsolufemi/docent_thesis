Well, the main point of this painting is to capture a fashionable, "Orientalist" scene, which was quite a trend back then. It's essentially a study of animals, showing a hierarchy of intelligence; if you look, the animals get "smarter" the higher up the canvas they are—starting with the mare and foal, then the greyhounds, and finally the monkeys at the top. As for the human element, the owner isn't actually in the frame, but you can tell they're there because of those two pipes in the jar over on the right.
