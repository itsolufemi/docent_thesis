Well, basically, this is Landseer's take on "Orientalism," which was a huge fashion trend back in the Victorian era. He's creating a scene of luxury and exoticism, but if you look closely, there's a bit of a playful hierarchy going on with the animals. I'd say the point is to show a progression of intelligence, since the animals get "smarter" the higher up the canvas they are—starting with the mare and foal at the bottom and ending with those monkeys at the top.
