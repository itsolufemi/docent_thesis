Well, this painting is really about Landseer playing with the "Orientalist" trend that was all the rage back in the Victorian era. The main point is actually a bit of a clever joke about intelligence; if you look, the animals get smarter the higher up the canvas they are. You've got the mare and foal at the bottom, then the greyhounds, and finally the monkeys at the top—one of whom is even wearing an earring!
