Well, this one's a bit of a rarity for Landseer, who usually stuck to British scenes, but here he's playing with "Orientalist" themes. The point of the piece is really to showcase a luxurious, exotic scene, focusing on the bond between animals. He tells the story through a hierarchy of intelligence—if you look, the animals actually get smarter the higher up the canvas they are, moving from the mare and foal at the bottom up to the monkeys at the top.
