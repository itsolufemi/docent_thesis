So, the main point of this painting is really about Landseer playing with the "Orientalist" trends that were all the rage in Victorian England. He's telling a story of luxury and exoticism, and he does it by layering the animals—from the mare and foal at the bottom up to the monkeys at the top—to show a progression of intelligence. It's actually a bit of a puzzle; the owner isn't even in the frame, but you can tell they're right there because of the two pipes in the jar over on the right.
