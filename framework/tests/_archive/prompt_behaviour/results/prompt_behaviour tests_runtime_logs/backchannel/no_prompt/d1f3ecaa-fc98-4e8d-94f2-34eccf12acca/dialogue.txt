Well, this one is actually quite a rarity for Landseer. He was the most fashionable painter of the Victorian era, but he didn't usually do "Orientalist" themes like this. The painting is really about a hierarchy of intelligence; if you look at the animals, they get smarter the higher up the canvas they are, moving from the mare and foal at the bottom up to the monkeys at the top.
