Right, so this painting is basically a study of animals, but there's a clever bit of storytelling in how Landseer arranged them. The main point is a sort of hierarchy of intelligence; as your eye moves from the bottom of the canvas to the top, the animals actually become more "intelligent." You've got the mare and foal down low, then those Persian greyhounds, and finally the monkeys up at the top—one of whom is even wearing an earring.
