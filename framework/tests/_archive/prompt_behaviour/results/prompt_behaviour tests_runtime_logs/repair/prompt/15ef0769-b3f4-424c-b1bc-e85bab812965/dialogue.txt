Well, the interesting thing is that there aren't actually any people *in* the painting, but you can tell someone's there because of the two pipes in the jar on the right. 

As for who's connected to it in real life, it was painted by Edwin Landseer, who was a huge favourite of Queen Victoria. Then there's the bit of drama with the ownership; Sir Richard Wallace bought it from the Prince of Wales for a massive sum—about £6,300—which was one of the most expensive things he ever bought. Actually, it's thought the Prince might have taken advantage of Wallace's generosity because he was always short on cash.
