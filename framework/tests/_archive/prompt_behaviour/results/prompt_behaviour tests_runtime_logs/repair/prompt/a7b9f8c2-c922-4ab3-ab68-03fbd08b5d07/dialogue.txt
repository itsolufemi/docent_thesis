Well, this piece is really about the "exotic" appeal of the East, which was all the rage in Victorian England. Landseer tells the story by arranging the animals in a sort of hierarchy of intelligence; you've got the mare and foal at the bottom, the greyhounds above them, and then the monkeys right at the top. 

Oh, and if you look at the jar on the right, you'll see two pipes—that's how he suggests the owner is nearby without actually painting him into the scene.
