So, the main point here is really about "Orientalism"—which was just a huge fashion in Victorian England. Landseer is using the tent and the animals to create this exotic, luxurious atmosphere. He's actually playing a bit of a game with the animals, too; if you look, they get more "intelligent" the higher up the painting they are, from the mare and foal at the bottom up to the monkeys at the top.
