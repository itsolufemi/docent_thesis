Text:
Well, this one is actually quite a bit of a rarity for Landseer, who was the absolute toast of the Victorian era. It's important because it shows him leaning into 'Orientalism', which was a huge fashion at the time—basically a Western fascination with the Middle East and Asia. 

He tells the story by layering the animals; if you look, they get more 'intelligent' the higher up the canvas you go, from the horses up to the monkeys. Right over there on the side, you can see two pipes in a jar, which is his clever way of suggesting the owner is present without actually painting him.
