Well, the interesting thing about the visual hierarchy here is that Landseer has actually arranged the animals by their perceived intelligence. If you look at the painting, the "smarter" the animal is, the higher up in the composition it's placed. So, you've got the mare and foal at the bottom, then the greyhounds, and finally the monkeys at the top—one of whom is even wearing an earring!
