Well, it's basically a showcase of "Orientalist" fashion and luxury from the Victorian era. Landseer is using these animals—the mare and foal, the greyhounds, and the monkeys—to create a sort of hierarchy of intelligence, with the smartest animals placed higher up in the composition. Oh, and if you look at the jar on the right, you'll see two pipes, which is the artist's subtle way of telling us the owner is actually right there, just out of frame.
