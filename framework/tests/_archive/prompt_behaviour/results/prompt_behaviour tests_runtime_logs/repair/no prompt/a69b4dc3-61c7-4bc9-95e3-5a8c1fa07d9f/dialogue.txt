So, this is really a study of animals and a bit of "Orientalist" fashion from the Victorian era. It's essentially a portrait of a mare and her foal resting under a tent, but if you look closer, Landseer has added a little hierarchy to the scene. He's placed the animals in order of intelligence—the horses at the bottom, then the greyhounds, and the monkeys right at the top.

Oh, and if you're wondering where the humans are, just look at that jar on the right with the two pipes; that's how we know the owner is nearby.
