Well, basically, it's a bit of a "status" painting. Landseer is showing off this exotic, luxurious scene to lean into the Orientalism that was really trendy back then. He tells the story through a bit of a visual ladder—the higher up the painting you look, the more "intelligent" the animals get, going from the mare and foal at the bottom up to those monkeys at the top.
