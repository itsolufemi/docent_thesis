Well, this painting is actually a bit of a rarity for Landseer. He was the most famous animal painter of the Victorian era, but he rarely dipped into "Orientalism"—that whole fashionable trend of painting exotic scenes from the Middle East. 

The story here is more about a hierarchy of nature than a plot; if you look closely, the animals get more "intelligent" as you move up the canvas, from the mare and foal at the bottom up to the monkeys at the top. It's a bit of a play on status and nature.
