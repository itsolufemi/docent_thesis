Well, the main thing to know is that this was a real status symbol. It's a rare example of Landseer diving into 'Orientalism', which was incredibly trendy back then, focusing on the exoticism of the East.

What's actually quite fascinating is the price tag—Sir Richard Wallace paid a massive £6,300 for it. He bought it from the Prince of Wales, who was often short on cash and likely took advantage of Wallace's desire to climb the social ladder.
