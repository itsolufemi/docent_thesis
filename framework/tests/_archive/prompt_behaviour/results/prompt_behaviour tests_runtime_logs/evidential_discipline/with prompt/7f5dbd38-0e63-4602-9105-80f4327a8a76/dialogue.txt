Well, this piece is really about Landseer playing with the "Orientalist" trends that were all the rage back in the Victorian era. To me, the main point is a bit of a clever commentary on intelligence and status. If you look at the animals, he's arranged them so that as you look higher up the painting, the animals actually become more intelligent—starting with the mare and foal at the bottom, then the greyhounds, and finally the monkeys at the top.
