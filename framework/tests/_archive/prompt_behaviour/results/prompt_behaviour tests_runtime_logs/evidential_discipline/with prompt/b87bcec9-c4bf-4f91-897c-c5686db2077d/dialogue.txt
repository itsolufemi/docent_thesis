Well, if you look at how Landseer's placed the animals, there's actually a bit of a hierarchy going on. He's arranged them so that the higher up the painting you look, the more intelligent the animals become. It starts with the mare and foal at the bottom, then the greyhounds, and finally the monkeys at the top.
