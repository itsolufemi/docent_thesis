Well, it's actually a bit of a visual joke about intelligence. Landseer arranged the animals so that as your eye moves higher up the painting, the animals get "smarter"—starting with the horses at the bottom, then the greyhounds, and finally the monkeys at the top. I'd read it as a playful way of ranking the creatures in the scene.
