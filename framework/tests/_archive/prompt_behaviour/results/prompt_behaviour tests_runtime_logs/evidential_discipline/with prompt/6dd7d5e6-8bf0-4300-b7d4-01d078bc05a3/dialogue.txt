Well, if you look closely, there's actually a bit of a hierarchy going on here. The animals are arranged so that the higher up they are in the painting, the more intelligent they are meant to be. You've got the mare and foal at the bottom, then the greyhounds, and finally the monkeys at the top. I'd read it as Landseer's way of adding a clever little intellectual layer to the scene.
