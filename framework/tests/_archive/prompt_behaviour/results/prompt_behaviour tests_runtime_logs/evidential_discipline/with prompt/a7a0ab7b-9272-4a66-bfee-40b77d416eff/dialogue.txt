Since you haven't mentioned which one we're looking at, I'm guessing you mean the portrait of Margaret, Countess of Blessington? Well, it's actually a bit of a contradiction. She's dressed quite simply with very little jewellery, but then you've got that ermine-trimmed robe draped over the chair, which is a huge giveaway of her aristocratic status. I'd read it as a deliberate choice by Lawrence to balance a sense of natural beauty with the social standing she held.
