Well, it's a clever little detail to show a hierarchy of intelligence. If you look closely, Landseer has arranged the animals so that the higher up they are in the painting, the more intelligent they are. Since the monkeys are at the top and one's even wearing jewelry, it's his way of marking them as the "smartest" of the bunch.
