Well, it's actually a bit of a clever visual joke by Landseer. He's arranged the animals so that the higher up they are in the painting, the more "intelligent" they're considered to be. So, you've got the mare and foal at the bottom, and the monkeys perched higher up as the smartest of the bunch.
