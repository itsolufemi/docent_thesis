Well, it's more of a peek into the "Orientalist" fashion of the time rather than a documentary. Landseer is basically playing with a romanticised fantasy here—the fancy tent, the exotic animals—to create a scene that felt luxurious and mysterious to a Victorian audience. It's less about factual representation and more about the allure of the "East."
